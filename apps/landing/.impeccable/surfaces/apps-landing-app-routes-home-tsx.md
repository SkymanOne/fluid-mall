## Scope

Landing page for Fluid (`apps/landing`, one route). Persuade mode.

## Audience and job

Mass market UK fashion shoppers, and hackathon judges who see it before the live demo. They should get what Fluid is in one viewport and open the web app. Proof is the mechanism shown with fictional sample pieces from the made up shops in the compose mock, always labelled as samples. No customers, press, metrics or partners exist, so none are shown.

## Constraints

Same brand as the web app studio theme (stock, ink, cobalt, Sofia Sans family, 2px corners), with more expression. Primary CTA opens the web app at `VITE_WEB_APP_URL`. Photos are Grok generated and fictional. No real brands.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A fashion campaign where every piece wears its swing tag, and the tag carries the shop's own facts. Refuses the app landing template of phone mockup plus feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Stock #F5F4F0 ground, ink #121212, cobalt #2F45FF only on what is yours (CTA, bag). The bag section is drenched cobalt. Swing tags: stock card, punched hole, ink string. A woven care label states the truth rules. Sofia Sans Extra Condensed numerals at poster scale, Condensed headings, square 2px corners.

STORY: Say what you want. Fluid searches UK shops and builds a shop around it. Ask to reshape it. One bag across shops, split per shop at checkout. Facts come from the shop. Open Fluid.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim nav, wordmark left, cobalt Open Fluid right. Left half: H1 at display scale, a prompt line typing an intent, cobalt CTA. Right half: full height campaign photo with four swing tags pinned to the garments, shop, price and delivery on each, labelled sample.

FORM: Swing tags and care labels, position 5 of 7, seed 4538dada. Signature interaction: chips reshape a sample shop (grid, carousel, list, dark) with view transitions while items stay put.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
